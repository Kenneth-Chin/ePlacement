# Public-launch legal and safety review

> This is a practical risk review, not legal advice. Obtain advice from a Malaysian intellectual-property lawyer before a large public launch.

Reviewed: 1 October 2026.

## Current safeguards

- The Malaysian coat of arms and government logos are not present in the public snapshot.
- Candidate identity fields contain unmistakably fictional practice values.
- The interface contains one persistent notice identifying it as a practice website rather than the official ePlacement website.
- No government login, password, MyKad number, or other sensitive credential is requested.
- The leaderboard is stored only in the user's browser and requests a nickname.
- The repository contains no application secrets or server credentials.

## Remaining publication risks

1. **Clinic-data rights remain unresolved.** The GIReT/DIS directory identifies the Oral Health Programme, KKM as its copyright holder, and no explicit open-data licence was found for the collected directory/API snapshot. Obtain written permission or replace the dataset with explicitly open-licensed data. The clinic dataset is excluded from this repository's MIT licence.
2. **The name and visual similarity require review.** The project is named **ePlacement Practice** and intentionally rehearses an official workflow. Confirm that the final name, domain, search description, and visual presentation do not create a likelihood of confusion.
3. **Original instruction wording is present.** The simulation includes Malay placement and acknowledgement wording modelled on the official flow. Confirm permission or replace substantial copied wording before broad promotion.
4. **Do not reintroduce restricted material.** Do not add the Malaysian coat of arms, ministry logos, official screenshots, real candidate information, or government credentials.
5. **A global leaderboard would require privacy controls.** Before adding one, publish a privacy notice and provide moderation, rate limiting, retention, access/deletion, and abuse-reporting processes. Collect nicknames only.

## Relevant primary/official material

- Malaysian Government guidance describes the National Coat of Arms as the official emblem of the government: https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/government/learn-about-malaysia/coat-of-arms-of-malaysia
- The National Security Council states that the Federal Coat of Arms is restricted to federal ministries, departments, and officials on official business, and describes penalties for unauthorized use under Act 414: https://www.mkn.gov.my/web/ms/2023/02/03/jata-persekutuan-pemakaian-lambang-kerajaan-persekutuan/
- The 2017 amendment to the Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act 1963 sets the current section 5 penalty: https://www.istiadat.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/EMBLEMS-AND-NAMES-PREVENTION-OF-IMPROPER-USE-AMENDMENT-ACT-2017.pdf
- MyIPO publishes the Copyright Act 1987: https://www.myipo.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/Copyright-Act-1987-Act-332.pdf
- MyIPO publishes the Trade Marks Act 2019 and current materials: https://www.myipo.gov.my/trademark-act/
- The Personal Data Protection Commissioner explains the PDPA and current guidance: https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/pdp-act-2010-en/
- The Commissioner's privacy-notice guidance says a written notice is mandatory for data users processing personal data in scope: https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/akta/panduan-penyediaannotis-perlindungan-data-peribadi/
- The source DIS:CLINICAL page identifies its copyright holder: https://dis.moh.gov.my/clinical/
