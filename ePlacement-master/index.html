Practise the placement rush before the real day.

Rehearse the time-sensitive clinic selection flow using a snapshot of publicly listed Malaysian dental clinic data.

How it works

One short rehearsal, four steps

  1. 01
    Choose a nickname

    Use an alias, not your legal name. Results currently stay in this browser only.

  2. 02
    Start the countdown

    The simulated session begins ten seconds before clinic selection opens.

  3. 03
    Open Penempatan Calon

    Use the sidebar when the clock reaches noon, just as you would navigate during the real workflow.

  4. 04
    Review and confirm

    Complete the acknowledgement to record your practice time, then retry to improve.
